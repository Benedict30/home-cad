Layout A · now

Bunk room in Bedroom 3, toddler with you

Bedroom 3: older two (bunk + desk for two). Bedroom 2: helper. Main: you + toddler's cot. No walls removed.

  • Pros: no hacking, every kid has a desk spot today, helper's room is a proper room with a window.
  • Cons: Bedroom 3 is full; once the toddler moves in, there's no floor left for a third bed and both desks.

Layout B · after (toddler ready to share, ~3–4)

Kids take the main bedroom

Main bedroom: all three kids, desk for two, their own bathroom. Bedroom 3: you (queen). Bedroom 2: helper. No walls removed.

  • Pros: biggest room for the most people, desks stay put, room to add a bookshelf divider when they're older.
  • Cons: you lose the ensuite and share Bath 2 with the helper; queen instead of king.

Layout C

Sleep room + homework wall in the living room

Bedroom 3: all three kids sleep (bunk + toddler bed). Desk for two on the living-room window wall. You keep the main bedroom. No walls removed.

  • Pros: toddler naps aren't disturbed by homework; you can supervise from the kitchen; you keep the ensuite.
  • Cons: TV and dining noise at homework time; the living room loses a wall to desks.
D

Layout D

Kids next to you, helper by the living room

Bedroom 2: older two (bunk + desk for two). Bedroom 3: helper. Main: you + toddler's cot. No walls removed.

  • Pros: kids share a wall with your room; helper's room is on the living/kitchen side, giving everyone more privacy.
  • Cons: kids get the smaller room (2.0 m desk instead of 2.4 m) and the helper gets the bigger one.

Before any work starts

  • None of these layouts hacks a wall. If you later change your mind, your DRC contractor applies for the HDB permit, and a Professional Engineer is needed for any reinforced-concrete wall.
  • Find out the block's completion date: bathroom walls and floors can't be touched for the first 3 years.
  • Check walls for switches, pipes and trunking, and the ceiling for beams, before planning built-ins.
  • Measure on site before ordering furniture, especially the bunk, the desks and the wardrobes.
  • These plans are read from a photo of the plan. Check every size against the official HDB floor plan.
