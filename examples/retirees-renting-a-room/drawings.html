Layouts

Each layout has a Now and an After renting plan. All four keep every wall: you need all three bedrooms, and a rented room has to stay an original bedroom.

ATenant up front

RecommendedWalls down: none

+The tenant’s door is the first off the corridor, the studio sits between their room and yours, and your bathroom is two steps from your bed.

−The rented room backs onto the living room, so the TV goes on the far window wall. The studio gets the smaller bedroom.

BBigger studio

Walls down: none

+About 10% more floor for painting, with space for a sofa bed and a plan chest.

−The tenant sleeps on the other side of your bedroom wall (W5), and the smaller room brings in less rent.

CRent the master suite

Walls down: none

+The highest rent, you never share a bathroom with the tenant, and the studio sits between you.

−You give up the biggest room and your own bathroom. There is only 0.6–0.8 m beside your bed, and the tenant walks past your door.

DStudio in the living room

Walls down: none

+Keeps a proper guest room for visiting family, and paints in the best daylight in the flat.

−Paint, smell and clutter sit in the room you share with the tenant, and the living room gets tighter.

In every layout

Easy to get around

  • A clear Ø1.5 m turning space in the living room and your bedroom
  • Night lights from your bed to the bathroom and along the 1.1 m corridor
  • Shoe cabinet with a seat at the door, chairs with armrests, and no rugs
  • EASE: grab bars, slip-resistant treatment, a fold-down shower seat and ramps, if one of you is 65+ (or 60–64 and needs help with daily activities)
  • The bedroom doors look about 850 mm wide. If you ever need a wheelchair, widening them to 900 mm clear means cutting into W2/W4, which needs HDB approval.

Dog, studio and storage

  • A slip-resistant, scratch-resistant floor works for you and the dog
  • A top-hung glass slider at the open kitchen keeps wok smoke in and the dog out, with no gate or floor track to trip on
  • A waist-height wash tub in the yard for dog baths and brushes, plus a washer-dryer stack and a utility cabinet
  • The shelter is your storeroom: free-standing racks for canvases. Keep solvents out of it.
  • Aircon condensers sit on the ledge behind Bath 1. Plan the trunking routes before any carpentry.

These plans are read from a photo of your HDB floor plan. Dimensions run to wall centre-lines, so the usable space is about 100–200 mm smaller. Check everything against the official plan and measure on site before you buy furniture or do any work.
