HDB layout plans

4-room flat, about 93 m². Couple working from home, baby next year, Mum may move in. Drawn to scale from a photo of the HDB plan: dimensions are to wall centre-lines, so usable space is about 100–200 mm less. Check against the official plan and measure on site.

Best fit: Layout 1, rotate the rooms

Two separate offices now, so you can both take calls. When the baby and your mum arrive, Mum takes Bedroom 3 (the bigger room, near the common bathroom), the cot sits in your room for the first year or two, and Bedroom 2 becomes a shared office with a bookshelf divider. Nothing gets knocked down: you will need all three bedrooms.

Later, when your child needs Bedroom 2 (around age 2), it becomes the nursery and the office moves to the glass study in Layout 2.

Before any hacking

  • Permit: your HDB-registered contractor applies to HDB; a Professional Engineer is needed if a wall is reinforced concrete.
  • Completion date of your block: bathroom walls and floors can't be hacked in the first 3 years.
  • Switches, pipes and aircon trunking inside the walls, and beams above them.
  • Measure on site before ordering carpentry. This was read from a photo of the plan.
