The prompt

Works best with a clear, straight photo of the plan
I've attached the floor plan of my HDB flat. Be my Singapore interior designer and help me plan the renovation.

About us:
- Who lives here: [e.g. couple + 1 toddler, both work from home, 1 cat]
- Style we like: [e.g. Japandi, Scandinavian, modern, industrial]
- Must-haves: [e.g. dining for 6, a home office, lots of storage]
- Budget: [e.g. $50k]

Please:
1. Read the plan. List every room with its rough size, and point out the windows, the household shelter, the kitchen, the service yard and the bathrooms.
2. Wall check. Go through every internal wall and say if it can be knocked down, using HDB's rules:
   - Structural walls (shaded black or marked on the plan's legend) must stay.
   - Household shelter walls must stay.
   - Bathroom walls and floor finishes can't be hacked for the first 3 years after the block is completed.
   - Other thin partitions can go with HDB approval and an HDB-registered (DRC) contractor; reinforced concrete ones need a Professional Engineer.
   Tell me which walls you're unsure about and how to check.
3. Give me 4 layout ideas that suit our household. Include at least one that knocks down nothing. For each: which walls come down, what each room becomes, furniture that fits (sizes in mm), and the pros and cons.
4. Suggest colours, flooring and 3 key built-ins for our style. Keep it practical for Singapore living, and cover shoe storage at the door, the shelter as a storeroom, the service yard, aircon, humidity and wok cooking.
5. Draw each layout as a simple floor plan to scale: walls that must stay in black (bathroom and external walls too), walls that can go in green, removed walls as dashed lines, and furniture as labelled boxes.

End with what I should confirm with HDB or my contractor before any hacking.

What you get

  • Every room, read off your plan with rough sizes, windows, the shelter, kitchen, yard and bathrooms.
  • A wall-by-wall knock-down check using HDB’s rules: structural and shelter walls stay, bathroom walls wait 3 years, partitions need a permit.
  • 4 layouts for your household from a no-hacking option to bigger changes, with furniture sizes in mm and the pros and cons.
  • A style plan that works in Singapore colours, flooring and built-ins that handle shoes, humidity, aircon and wok cooking.
  • Floor plans you can look at each layout drawn to scale, with the walls colour-coded.
